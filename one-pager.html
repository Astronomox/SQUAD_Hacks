The Problem

Nigeria loses ₦200B+ annually to ghost workers — fake, deceased, or duplicate employees collecting government salaries. Manual audits catch less than 10% of fraud. Current systems verify names in databases, not whether humans actually exist.

Our Solution

VerifyAI uses AI to intercept salary disbursement. Funds are locked in a Squad escrow vault until each employee passes Isolation Forest anomaly detection and facial liveness verification. Only verified employees unlock Squad disbursement.

AI Technical Depth

Isolation Forest Model

200 estimators · 8% contamination · 6 behavioural features: enrollment hour, batch size, IP sharing, device reuse, salary ratio, attendance gap

Facial Liveness Verification

3-step verification: face detection → blink detection → identity match. Blocks photo/video spoofing attacks.
