The Problem

Nigeria loses ₦200B+ annually to ghost workers - fake, deceased, or duplicate employees collecting government salaries. Manual audits catch less than 10% of fraud. Current systems verify names in databases, not whether the person actually exists.

Our Solution

VerifyAI intercepts salary disbursement before any funds move. Payroll is locked in a Squad Virtual Account escrow until each employee passes Isolation Forest anomaly detection and 3-step facial liveness verification. Only cleared employees unlock Squad disbursement.

AI Technical Depth

Isolation Forest - Python FastAPI

200 estimators, 8% contamination, 6 behavioural features: enrollment hour, batch size, IP sharing, device reuse, salary ratio, attendance gap. Real inference on every scan. Risk score 0 to 100 per employee.

Facial Liveness Verification

3-step check: face detection, blink detection, identity match. Blocks photo and video spoofing. Fires Squad Transfer only on pass. Trust score returned per verification.
